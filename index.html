Who Is Michael Serdan?

First and foremost, I’m a concerned father and a husband. I’m married and, like many of you, I have two school-aged kids attending public schools in Windsor. I want our schools to better reflect the needs of these kids and their families.

Having travelled a bit and having experienced our system as well as others, I’m concerned about my kids entering the outside world unprepared. That concern is why I’m running for school board trustee.

My Background

  • University of Windsor degrees in Psychology (’89) and Business Administration (’92), plus several professional course programs
  • Extensive international work, teaching and business experience, having lived and worked in Japan and China
  • Union experience with CUPE and CAW: shop steward, union delegate and health and safety representative
  • Minor hockey coach with WMHA and RMHA; referee with OMHA and WRA; umpire with WUA

What I Stand For

Parental Rights

Parents need to know, and be kept fully informed about, everything that comes up regarding their child, as soon as the issue arises.

Parents love their kids the most and know them best, having been with them from birth. They are also responsible for their children’s physical, mental and financial well-being until the age of maturity, 18.

Safe Schools

Schools that are safe and accepting for all students and teachers alike.

If a student has a special need or accommodation that must be considered, it should be made available without infringing on the rights or consideration of other students. A safe place for everyone.

Zero Tolerance for Bullying and Violence

Bullying is a pervasive problem that has existed in education since the very first school, and it exists in the adult world too. It takes many forms: physical, sexual, verbal, social exclusion and psychological. None of these behaviours are okay, and some are profoundly illegal.

The impact on those targeted is serious: depression, anxiety and even PTSD, which can damage a child’s entire educational and professional career.

Education on Identifying and Dealing with Bullying

With social media everywhere, and bullying able to affect a life so deeply, students need to understand this behaviour and have strategies for processing it when it occurs.

Core and STEM Learning, With a Focus on Literacy and Less Dependence on Devices

Core and STEM subjects such as math and science should be the focal point of the educational experience. Our modern world comes from these foundations, and they are required for advanced study in almost every field, from applied science to the skilled trades. Literacy, the ability to read, write and understand, is the foundation of communication in every field.

There has been a movement toward audiobooks for teaching literature, calculators for doing math, and AI for answering questions and writing papers. That is a serious problem. Kids need to learn to accomplish tasks without their electronic helpers. The helpers should come only once fundamental skills have been mastered.

Financial Accountability

Funding is always short and is often clawed back. The money that is available should be spent wisely on what matters, and those responsible for spending it should be held accountable.

School Lunches, Special Education, Extracurriculars and Sports

As funding has been cut or frozen, many programs have been discontinued, paused or reduced. That’s not okay. Students who need special education should have those resources readily available. Sports and extracurriculars enrich education and teach socialization, fitness, teamwork and group dynamics, skills students will need beyond school.

Many households in our community are single-parent or less fortunate. School lunches help ensure children get the nutrition they need to succeed, so they should be available.

Educational Direction Through Counselling, Guidance and Business Mentoring

Our economy is changing quickly, and AI is becoming part of everything we do. Skilled trades are proving more important than ever, while poorly planned degrees are proving far less useful than the people who paid for them expected. Students need direction toward the job market they will actually face, not the one TV shows advertise.

A Province-Wide Coalition of School Boards and Concerned Parents

School trustees are elected by parents in communities across the province, a huge voting bloc. If boards work together with the media and show unity and solidarity of purpose, Queen’s Park will have no choice but to take their concerns, especially funding, seriously.

Strong Short-Term Solutions to Funding

Hopefully Queen’s Park will fund schools and all programs as it should. Until it does, turning to the business community is an option worth pursuing. For a business, helping fund a school sports team or school lunches is great PR, an altruistic act, great for the kids, and fully tax-deductible. It’s worth looking into.

Why I’m Running

This is what I believe is important within our schools. I have kids attending a public primary school in Windsor right now, just as many of you, the voters, do. I want them, and every student, ready for the world beyond the classroom.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
